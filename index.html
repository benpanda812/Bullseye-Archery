The numbers on the target are the points for each ring. Wind pushes the arrow. Streaks of bullseyes multiply points. Beat the goal to clear a level (every 5th is a boss). Easy mode shows what your shot would score; Hard and Daily give no hints. Daily uses the same setup for everyone each day. On a keyboard: ↑↓ aim, ←→ power, Space to fire.
